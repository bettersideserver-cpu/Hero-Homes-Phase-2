Visitor analytics

When IP analytics is enabled, Hero Homes records the pages and rooms you view, browsing session times, and your internet connection's IP address.

We use this information to understand where website visits come from and how visitors explore the property. If you submit your contact details, your browsing activity can be associated with that registration.

Analytics is stored using Supabase. Administrators can open an IPinfo lookup link to check an IP address's approximate location. Opening that link shares the IP address with IPinfo. No automatic third-party location lookup runs during your visit. We do not request GPS location.

IP-based location is approximate. VPNs, shared connections and mobile networks may show a different city. Location data may also be unavailable.

Records are available to signed-in dashboard users. Site administrators can delete visitor analytics and associated IP/location records. New visits may create new records after deletion.

For questions about your visitor details or deletion requests, contact the Hero Homes team through the contact options on the website.
